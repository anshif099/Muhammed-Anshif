Pictured: the subject, in his natural habitat.

He enjoys the entire process — taking a rough idea, turning it into something tangible, and pushing it all the way to production. From interfaces and interactions to backend systems and deployment, he likes being involved from front to back.

His toolkit spans modern web technologies, with a focus on React, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, databases, APIs, and cloud platforms. The exact tools may change from project to project, but the objective stays the same: build things that are useful, reliable, and ready to ship.

Whether it’s a website, web application, mobile experience, or something more ambitious, he approaches each build with equal parts engineering and curiosity — because the interesting part is always seeing an idea become real.
