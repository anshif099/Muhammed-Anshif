A fragrance storefront made for discovery

A refined UAE perfume storefront that turns product discovery, collection browsing, and purchasing into one focused digital experience. Filed as a solo build.

By The Investigation Desk · 2025

A UAE-based fragrance storefront built as more than a catalogue: a polished digital experience for discovering and purchasing perfumes. The build puts the products first, pairing an elegant visual language with responsive layouts, detailed product presentation, collection browsing, and a streamlined shopping journey.

Every part of the interface is arranged around the product. Collections remain easy to scan, individual fragrances have room to tell their story, and the path from discovery to purchase stays clear across desktop and mobile.

The result is a modern perfume experience designed to make browsing feel as refined as the fragrances themselves.

“The products stay at the centre; the interface makes room for discovery.”

Built with React and TypeScript, with Tailwind CSS bringing the interface together across desktop and mobile. The component-driven build keeps the storefront consistent while allowing its product presentation and collection views to adapt cleanly at every screen size.

E-commerce behaviour, product catalogue structure, and responsive interactions were treated as parts of the same experience. The visual polish supports the shopping journey instead of competing with it.

Beek Perfumes is in production at beekperfumes.ae. The evidence is available to inspect directly from the link below.
