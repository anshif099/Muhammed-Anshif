Case File · Exhibit D · ben10hits.vercel.app

Social proof, delivered post by post

A modern social engagement storefront that makes selecting pay-per-post Instagram services fast and straightforward. Filed as a solo build.

By The Investigation Desk · 2025

A social engagement platform built around organic-looking Instagram growth. Ben10Hits provides premium engagement services, connecting posts with comments and likes from artists, producers, DJs, influencers, and other social accounts.

The service is presented as a clear storefront. Visitors can understand the available engagement options, select the right service for a post, and move through the order flow without navigating an oversized account system.

Its pay-per-post model avoids subscriptions, keeping the commitment simple while giving users a direct route to building visible social proof.

“Choose the post, select the service, and keep the process moving.”

The experience combines a clean, modern interface with automated engagement workflows designed to make the process fast and straightforward.

React and TypeScript provide the storefront structure and application logic, while Tailwind CSS keeps the service catalogue and ordering experience consistent across desktop and mobile.

Ben10Hits is published at ben10hits.vercel.app. The live platform can be inspected directly from the evidence link below.
