A bold identity reduced to its strongest signals

A high-contrast digital experience where oversized typography, focused interactions, and controlled motion put the RDX brand at the centre. Filed as a solo build.

By The Investigation Desk · 2026

A bold digital experience for a modern creative technology company, built around a strong visual identity and an intentionally minimal interface. The site uses a high-contrast presentation, oversized typography, and focused interactions to introduce the RDX brand and its digital presence.

The identity establishes itself through scale and restraint. Strong type carries the message while a reduced interface keeps attention on the company, its attitude, and its creative direction.

Navigation remains direct, giving the experience enough structure without diluting the deliberate visual impact.

“Minimal structure, maximum identity.”

The experience keeps the interface deliberately simple — putting the brand at the centre while using motion, navigation, and carefully controlled layouts to create a distinctive agency-style presentation.

React and TypeScript support the interactive design beneath the surface. Responsive layouts preserve the oversized presentation and focused hierarchy as the experience moves between desktop and mobile.

RDX Company is published at rdxcompany.vercel.app. The live presentation can be inspected directly from the evidence link below.
