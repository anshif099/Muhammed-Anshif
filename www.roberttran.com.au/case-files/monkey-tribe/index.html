AI education built for the real world

An education and training platform that turns emerging AI technology into practical professional skills. Filed as a solo build.

By The Investigation Desk · 2026

An AI education and training platform built to turn emerging technology into practical skills. From prompt engineering and AI-powered workflows to digital marketing, brand building, and persuasive communication, Monkey Tribe brings real-world creative and advertising expertise into structured learning programmes.

The platform presents four specialised programmes — PromptX, GrowthX, BrandX, and CopyCraft — with a clear focus on practical application rather than AI theory.

Each programme gives a distinct area of work its own path. PromptX centres prompt engineering and AI-powered workflows; GrowthX connects AI with digital marketing; BrandX focuses on brand building and strategy; and CopyCraft develops persuasive creative communication.

“Less AI theory. More useful work.”

Built as the education and training division of Creative Monkeys Advertising, the experience is designed for professionals learning to work with AI in the real world.

The interface keeps the programme structure clear while giving the subject matter a confident creative identity. Visitors can understand the focus of each course, compare the available paths, and move toward the programme that fits their work.

Monkey Tribe is filed as a solo project and entered in 2026. The live platform can be inspected from the evidence link below.
