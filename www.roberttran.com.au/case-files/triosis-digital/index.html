Case File · Exhibit G · triosis.in

A digital agency positioned for measurable growth

A confident agency experience that brings strategy, creative marketing, technology, and business solutions into one focused digital presence. Filed as a solo build.

By The Investigation Desk · 2026

A digital agency website built to present Triosis as a strategic technology and marketing partner for businesses ready to grow. The experience brings together digital strategy, creative marketing, technology, and measurable business solutions through a clean and confident visual system.

Services are organised to make the agency’s capabilities easy to understand. Each part of the experience supports a clear route from discovering what Triosis offers to starting a direct client conversation.

Portfolio storytelling adds proof behind the positioning, while content publishing gives the agency a structured place to share thinking, expertise, and ongoing work.

“Strategy, technology, and creative work aligned around business growth.”

The site is designed around clear service presentation, portfolio storytelling, content publishing, and direct client conversion — giving businesses a focused way to understand the agency, its capabilities, and the work behind them.

Next.js and TypeScript provide the application foundation, Tailwind CSS maintains the responsive visual system, and Firebase supports the platform behind its published content and digital workflows.

Triosis Digital is published at triosis.in. The live agency experience can be inspected directly from the evidence link below.
